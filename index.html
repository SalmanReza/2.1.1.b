How long a non-Fard phrase stays
Fard phrases keep their fixed times. These numbers are seconds, and they apply only to Sunnah, Nafl and voluntary prayers.
How long the reward text for a voluntary prayer stays after you press Mark Complete. Whole seconds from 1 to 120. It does not change Fard recognition.

    
RECOVERY
Prayer Completion Recovery
Only for a prayer you performed and forgot to record. It is not a missed-prayer tool. The two Fard prayers just before it must already be recorded. Press and hold for three seconds, then confirm.
